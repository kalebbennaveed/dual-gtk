JavaScript animation of recorded, independently checked numerical simulations. Planning was performed offline; this webpage replays the results. The scene depicts sampled nominal position tubes, with the physical drone radius handled separately in the safety checks. These designed examples use bounded wind and bounded measurement noise; they are not a claim of a universal performance advantage.
